Seira

Live e-commerce brand, built end to end.

E-commerceAdmin PanelDashboardsLive Product

Seira is a live e-commerce business selling beach essentials in Egypt, and I built it end to end: the customer storefront, the back office behind it and the reporting on top. The store runs in Egyptian pounds with an Arabic option. The admin panel and dashboards are what let the business run day to day, not just look good online.

Highlights

  • Live storefront: car seat covers, head shades, beach racket sets
  • Complex admin panel for running the business behind the store
  • Dashboards for tracking the business
  • Built end to end, from product decisions to launch

Gems

Real-time football card game for online rooms.

MultiplayerReal-timeNext.jsTypeScript

Gems is a football card game played in real time with friends in online rooms. It has several game modes, so the same room mechanics support different ways to play. I built it as a web app that runs in the browser with nothing to install.

SS

SmartSecure

Smart locker booking app: find, book and unlock. My graduation project.

Graduation ProjectFlutterNode.jsSupabasePostgreSQL

SmartSecure was my graduation project: a full-stack platform where users find a locker on a live map, book it by the hour or day, and unlock it with a one-time code or QR scan. I designed the data model and business rules, then built the Flutter app, the REST API and an admin side for the operator. It is a working end-to-end system rather than a mock-up.

Highlights

  • Live locker map with location-based search and directions
  • Bookings with overlap checks, extension, cancellation and delegated access
  • Wallet top-up and locker pricing by size, per hour and per day
  • OTP and QR unlock flow, plus in-app notifications and automatic overdue handling
  • Admin tools: stats, analytics, users, lockers, locations and pricing
  • Role-based access (customer, admin, technician), JWT auth, English, Arabic and French

04 · About

About

I come from a Business Information Systems background, which means I was trained to look at a business, its processes and its data at the same time. I like turning a business problem into something measurable, then into a practical solution.

My focus is data and BI: SQL and Python for the analysis, Power BI and Tableau for making it useful to the people who decide. I'm also a Teaching Assistant for the AI in Business course at AASTMT Cairo, which keeps me explaining ideas clearly.

Outside of analysis, I build products. Founding and shipping my own ventures gave me an entrepreneurial mindset and a practical understanding of what it takes to get from requirement to a working system.
