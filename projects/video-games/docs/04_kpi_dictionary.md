# 04 | KPI dictionary

| KPI | Meaning | Calculation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total sales (M) | Copies sold, millions | Sum of total_sales | Only rows with sales |
| Games | Rows with sales | Count of rows | A game on 2 consoles counts twice |
| Average sales per game (M) | Typical sales of one game | Total sales / games | Pulled up by a few hits, read next to the median |
| Median sales (M) | The "middle" game | Middle value of sales | 0.12M, much lower than the mean of 0.35M |
| Genre share | A genre's part of the market | Genre sales / total sales | |
| Regional share of a genre | How strong a genre is in a region | Genre sales in region / all sales in region | Shows local taste |
| Million-seller rate | Share of games at 1M or more | Games with sales >= 1M / games | 1,505 of 18,922 (8.0%) |
| Top-N share | How concentrated sales are | Sales of the top N games / total | Top 100 = 12.0% |
| Sales by score band | Average sales for each critic score band | Average sales inside each band | Only games with a score |
