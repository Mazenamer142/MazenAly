# 02 | Stakeholders

> **Scenario.** These roles are my own assumptions, not real people.

| Role | Interest | Influence | What they need |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio head (sponsor) | Pick games that pay back | High | A short list with reasons and risks |
| Head of publishing | Platform deals, timing | High | Which platforms have the audience |
| Creative director | Making games the team believes in | Medium | To not be forced into a genre just because it sold |
| Finance lead | Return on the budget | High | To know how spread out sales are (hits versus average) |
| Regional marketing lead | Launch plan per region | Medium | Where each genre sells |
| Data / BI analyst | A dashboard people can use | Medium | Clear definitions and a clean model |

## Tension to manage
Finance wants the safest genre by total sales. The creative director wants a genre where the team is strong. The dashboard should show the data for both views and let the sponsor choose, instead of hiding one of them.

## How I would work with them
- One short workshop with the sponsor and finance to agree what "a good result" means (for example, "one game above 1 million copies").
- A review with marketing on the regional pages.
- Sign-off of the KPI definitions before any build starts.
