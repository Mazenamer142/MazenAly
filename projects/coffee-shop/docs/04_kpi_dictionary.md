# 04 | KPI dictionary

| KPI | Meaning | How it is calculated | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Money from sales | Sum of quantity x unit price | No cost data, so this is revenue, not profit |
| Revenue per day | Average daily sales | Revenue / number of days with data | Use this to compare months of different length |
| Sale lines | Number of product lines sold | Count of rows | Not the same as receipts, there is no receipt id |
| Units | Items sold | Sum of quantity | |
| Revenue per line | Average value of a line | Revenue / sale lines | About $4.70 and flat, so growth is volume |
| Morning rush share | Share of revenue 7:00 to 10:59 | Revenue in those hours / total revenue | By store |
| Evening share | Share of revenue after 17:00 | Revenue from 17:00 on / total revenue | By store |
| Category share | Category revenue as % of total | Category revenue / total revenue | |
| Size mix | Small / regular / large split | Lines by size / lines of sized drinks | Only drinks have a size |
