# 02 | Stakeholders and RACI

> **Scenario.** These roles are my own assumptions, not real people.

## Who cares about this
| Role | What they want | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Growth without chaos, decisions backed by numbers | High |
| Operations manager | A staffing plan for each store and each hour | High |
| Store managers (x3) | To see their own store against the others | Medium |
| Head barista / menu lead | To know what sells and what does not | Medium |
| Finance (part time) | One agreed version of revenue | Medium |
| BI / data person | A clean model that refreshes | Medium |

## RACI for the dashboard
R = does the work, A = signs it off, C = asked for input, I = kept informed.

| Task | Owner | Ops manager | Store managers | Finance | BI analyst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agree the KPI definitions | A | C | C | R | C |
| Define peak hours and staffing rules | I | A | R | I | C |
| Build the data model | I | I | I | C | R / A |
| Build and test the dashboard | I | C | C | C | R / A |
| Check the numbers against the till reports | I | I | I | A | R |
| Weekly use of the dashboard | I | A | R | I | I |

## Tension to manage
The owner wants growth and longer opening hours. Store managers worry about tired staff. The dashboard should show the demand by hour and let people decide, not decide for them.
